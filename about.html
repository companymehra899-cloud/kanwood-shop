About Kanwood Interiors

WHO WE ARE

Manufacturer and dealer of office furniture and interiors.

Kanwood Interiors was set up in 2025, sister company of Bakshi Steel Ind. since 1960. Since its inception, we have established a reputation as a supplier of quality products. We focus on quality and aim to achieve total customer satisfaction — in the products we offer and in the service that follows.

Comfort and aesthetic design come before anything else. We create furniture for homes, offices, institutions, restaurants, patio, and workstations.
